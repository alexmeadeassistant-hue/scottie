## Executive Summary

The golf grip fitting market is experiencing a personalization boom. Over 35% of golfers in club fittings now request customized grips, and 2,500+ retail stores worldwide have added grip personalization services in the past year. GripScript's February 2026 retail launch timing aligns perfectly with industry trends identified at the 2026 PGA Show, where grip trainers and personalization were listed as a top 4 trend.

---

## Market Trends (2026)

### 1. Personalization Is King
- **35%+ of golfers** in club fittings request customized grips (PGA of America)
- **2,500+ retail stores** globally offer in-store grip personalization
- Average **25% markup** on personalized grip services
- Identified as **Top 4 trend** at 2026 PGA Show (alongside tech gear, limited-edition collabs, personalization)

### 2. Material & Design Innovation
- **Hybrid grips** combining rubber and cord materials gaining share
- **Non-taper/parallel designs** trending (Golf Pride Zero Taper launching Feb 17, 2026)
- **Ergonomic designs** focused on reducing hand fatigue
- **Softer compounds** for improved comfort and feel
- **Enhanced surface patterns** for better traction

### 3. Technology Integration
- **JumboMax** launched iOS grip fitting app using TrueDepth camera (21 data points)
- Emphasis on data-driven fitting vs. subjective feel
- Mobile grip replacement services expanding

### 4. Retail Expansion
- Major retailers (Golf Galaxy, PGA Tour Superstore) expanding fitting services
- Independent retailers adding grip customization to compete
- February 2026 is prime retail launch season (multiple new products launching)

## Competitive Landscape

### Major Players

**1. Golf Pride** (Market Leader)
- Products: Tour Velvet, MCC (MultiCompound), MCC Plus4, New Decade series
- Strength: Tour presence, brand recognition, innovation in cord/rubber hybrids
- Recent: Zero Taper putter grip launching Feb 17, 2026

**2. Lamkin**
- Products: Sink Fit, Fingerprint Technology, Genesis Material
- Strength: Focus on putter grips, ergonomic contoured shapes
- Positioning: Premium, performance-oriented

**3. SuperStroke**
- Strength: Putter grip dominance
- Known for oversized putter grips on tour

**4. JumboMax**
- Products: Oversized grips, grip fitting app
- Strength: Tech-enabled fitting (iOS app with TrueDepth camera)
- Positioning: "Never go back to skinny grips"

**5. Stick Grips**
- Positioning: Style + performance (colorful, custom designs)
- Appeal: Golfers wanting personality without sacrificing performance

**6. Emerging Players**
- WellMXS (newer entrant)
- Tackimac, Star (smaller players)
- Hireko Golf (custom component focus)
